## Compliance reminders for releases

- Preserve all applicable original copyright, attribution, trademark, and patent notices.
- Clearly mark upstream source files modified for Refabricated, as required by Apache-2.0 section 4(b).
- Keep the complete Apache License 2.0 text with redistributed builds, including
  any built JARs that contain Apache-licensed components.
- If an inherited component contains a relevant NOTICE file, preserve its applicable
  attribution text as required by Apache-2.0 section 4(d).
- Verify the licenses of additional code, libraries, fonts, textures, and assets
  actually bundled before publishing a release.

This document is a starting attribution record. It is not an exhaustive inventory
of all third-party components in future Refabricated releases.
